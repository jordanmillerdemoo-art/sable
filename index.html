07 /

Protocol questions

Know the boundary.

Is this live private inference?

No. This is an interactive frontend demonstration of a proposed private compute protocol. Answers, receipts and execution states are sample data.

What does the receipt establish?

This demo receipt establishes no real execution claim. A real design would define the evidence, its verification procedure and the exact scope of its claims.

Does shielded payment alone separate payer and prompt?

Shielded ZEC is part of the proposed access design. Keeping payment identity separate from requests also depends on protocol design and metadata handling. The interface illustrates that separation; it does not prove it.

Where is the prompt processed?

In the proposed design, inference uses plaintext within a trusted CPU/GPU execution boundary. The relay carries ciphertext. This frontend does not contact an enclave or model.

Can I stake or connect a wallet?

No. Both access modes are samples. No staking, reservation or ZEC transaction is performed.
